# About Me

I am **Dr. Irfan Qaisar**, and I received my Ph.D. in **Control Science and Engineering** from **Tsinghua University, Beijing, China**, in October 2026.

My doctoral research focused on intelligent methods for **occupancy sensing, occupancy prediction, occupant-centric control, and energy-efficient operation of smart buildings**. My dissertation was titled:

> *Multi-Sensor Occupancy Sensing for Occupant-Centric Control in Smart Buildings*

My research has progressively expanded from conventional machine learning and deep learning to **Transformers, Mamba-based sequence models, large language models, vision-language models, multimodal AI, and agentic AI**.

I am particularly interested in developing intelligent systems that connect:

**Sensing → Prediction → Reasoning → Decision Making → Building Control**

My work combines real-world multi-sensor and video data with **EnergyPlus, OpenStudio, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, LLMs, model predictive control, and reinforcement learning**.

The broader objective of my research is to develop **energy-efficient, autonomous, adaptive, and occupant-centered building systems** that balance energy performance with indoor environmental quality and thermal comfort.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.S. in **Control Theory and Control Engineering** at Nanjing University of Science and Technology and a B.E. in **Electronic Engineering** at Dawood University of Engineering and Technology.

My earlier research also includes robust control, fuzzy systems, event-triggered control, networked control systems, and multi-agent systems.
