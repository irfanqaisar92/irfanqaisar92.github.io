# Welcome

I am **Dr. Irfan Qaisar**, with a Ph.D. in Control Science and Engineering from **Tsinghua University, Beijing, China**.

My research focuses on **artificial intelligence for smart and sustainable built environments**, particularly at the intersection of occupant sensing, machine learning, large language models, intelligent control, and building energy systems.

My current research interests include **occupancy sensing and prediction, multimodal AI, large language models, agentic AI, occupant-centric HVAC control, building energy management, model predictive control, reinforcement learning, and building energy simulation**.

My research combines real-world building data, computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, foundation models, and physics-based simulation to develop intelligent, energy-efficient, and human-centered building systems.
